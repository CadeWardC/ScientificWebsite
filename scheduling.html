Scheduling

Build a calendar, click days to add tasks, then paste it into OneNote as an editable table.

How to use tasks and get this into OneNote
  1. Set the month, year, and options above — the preview updates automatically.
  2. Click any day cell in the calendar to add tasks for that day. Tasks are saved in this browser and shown in the day's cell.
  3. Click Copy Calendar for OneNote.
  4. In OneNote, click on the page where you want the calendar and press Ctrl+V.
  5. It pastes as a real OneNote table with your tasks in their days. Click any cell to keep typing more notes.

If the copy button fails (browser permissions), click Select Table and press Ctrl+C yourself, then paste into OneNote.

Tasks

    Press Enter to add. Tasks appear in this day's cell when you copy.
